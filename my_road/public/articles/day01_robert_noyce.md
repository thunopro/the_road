# Day 1: Robert Noyce (1927 – 1990)
*The Creator of Silicon Valley and the Microchip*

---

## ✍️ Copywork Passage (~520 words | Moderate B1–B2 Vocabulary, Smooth Sentence Flow)

Robert Noyce was one of the most important inventors and businessmen in modern history. People often called him the "Mayor of Silicon Valley" because he helped turn a quiet area of fruit farms in California into the technology capital of the world. Without his work, we would not have personal computers, smartphones, or the internet as we know them today.

Robert was born in Iowa in 1927. His father was a preacher, and his family lived a simple life. From a very young age, Robert was deeply curious about how machines worked. He loved fixing radios, building small planes, and experimenting with electronics in his basement. He was also a good student and an athlete. Later, he studied physics and earned a doctoral degree from MIT, one of the best science universities in the United States.

In 1956, Noyce moved to California to work for William Shockley, a famous scientist who had won a Nobel Prize for inventing the transistor. However, working with Shockley was very difficult. Shockley was suspicious of his employees, got angry easily, and did not listen to their opinions. Noyce and seven other young engineers decided they could no longer work in such an unhappy environment. Together, they made a brave decision to quit. People called them the "Traitorous Eight." In 1957, with the help of an investor, they started their own company called Fairchild Semiconductor.

At Fairchild, Noyce made his greatest invention: the silicon microchip, also known as the integrated circuit. Before this, electronic machines were huge and unreliable because thousands of components had to be connected by hand using fragile copper wires. Noyce found a clever solution. He figured out how to put many transistors, resistors, and electrical paths directly onto a single tiny piece of silicon. This idea made electronics much smaller, cheaper, and faster. It solved a massive problem and opened the door to the computer age.

In 1968, Noyce and his close friend Gordon Moore left Fairchild to create a new company named Intel. At Intel, Noyce wanted to build a different kind of workplace. In traditional companies, bosses worked in private luxury offices and wore formal suits, while normal workers stayed far below them. Noyce hated this separation. He created open offices with simple cubicles where everyone could talk freely. He wore casual clothes and treated his employees like teammates. He believed that the best ideas came from freedom, respect, and teamwork, not from strict rules.

Noyce was also famous for his positive attitude. He always told young people to be brave, take smart risks, and not worry about making mistakes. He believed that failure was just a normal step on the path to success. He even spent time giving advice to young inventors, including Steve Jobs, the co-founder of Apple.

Robert Noyce died suddenly from a heart attack in 1990 when he was sixty-two years old. Although his life ended early, his ideas live on. Every time you turn on a phone, use a computer, or send a message, you are using the technology that Robert Noyce helped create.

## 🔑 Useful Vocabulary & Phrases to Learn (Cụm từ thiết thực, dễ áp dụng)

1. **Turn [something] into [something]** (phrasal verb)
   - *Nghĩa*: Biến cái gì thành cái gì.
   - *Ví dụ*: He helped turn fruit farms into the technology capital (*Ông đã giúp biến những nông trại cây ăn quả thành thủ phủ công nghệ*).

2. **Be deeply curious about...** (phrase)
   - *Nghĩa*: Vô cùng tò mò / ham tìm hiểu về điều gì.
   - *Ví dụ*: He was deeply curious about how machines worked (*Cậu bé rất tò mò về cách máy móc vận hành*).

3. **Earn a doctoral degree** (collocation)
   - *Nghĩa*: Nhận / đạt được bằng tiến sĩ (thay vì nói chung chung *get a degree*).

4. **Be suspicious of someone** (phrase)
   - *Nghĩa*: Đa nghi, nghi ngờ ai đó.
   - *Ví dụ*: The manager was suspicious of his employees (*Người quản lý luôn nghi ngờ nhân viên của mình*).

5. **Make a brave decision** (collocation)
   - *Nghĩa*: Đưa ra một quyết định dũng cảm.
   - *Ví dụ*: They made a brave decision to quit (*Họ đã đưa ra quyết định dũng cảm là nghỉ việc*).

6. **Figure out [how to / what to]** (phrasal verb)
   - *Nghĩa*: Tìm ra cách / hiểu ra cách giải quyết một vấn đề.
   - *Ví dụ*: He figured out how to put components onto silicon (*Ông ấy đã tìm ra cách đặt các linh kiện lên tấm silicon*).

7. **Solve a massive problem** (collocation)
   - *Nghĩa*: Giải quyết một vấn đề rất lớn (*massive* = to lớn, khổng lồ).

8. **Open the door to...** (idiomatic phrase)
   - *Nghĩa*: Mở ra cánh cửa / mở đường cho một giai đoạn mới.
   - *Ví dụ*: It opened the door to the computer age (*Nó đã mở ra kỷ nguyên máy tính*).

9. **In traditional companies** (phrase)
   - *Nghĩa*: Ở các công ty truyền thống / kiểu cũ (rất hay dùng khi so sánh trong Writing).

10. **A different kind of workplace** (phrase)
    - *Nghĩa*: Một kiểu môi trường làm việc khác biệt.

11. **Strict rules** (collocation)
    - *Nghĩa*: Những quy tắc, luật lệ nghiêm ngặt / khắt khe.

12. **A positive attitude** (collocation)
    - *Nghĩa*: Thái độ tích cực, lạc quan trước cuộc sống.

13. **Take smart risks** (collocation)
    - *Nghĩa*: Dám chấp nhận rủi ro một cách khôn ngoan (không phải liều lĩnh mù quáng).

14. **On the path to success** (phrase)
    - *Nghĩa*: Trên con đường dẫn đến thành công.
    - *Ví dụ*: Mistakes are just steps on the path to success (*Sai lầm chỉ là những bước đi trên con đường thành công*).

15. **His ideas live on** (phrase)
    - *Nghĩa*: Ý tưởng / tư tưởng của ông ấy vẫn sống mãi (dùng khi nói về di sản của người đã khuất).
